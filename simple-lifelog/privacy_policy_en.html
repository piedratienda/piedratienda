Privacy Policy

Simple Life Log (the "App") / piedra tienda

Last updated: March 14, 2026

日本語 (Japanese)

1. Introduction

This App respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is stored and used.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter and Save in the App

The App stores the following data only on your device:

  • Life logs
    Date, selected items (e.g., exercise, reading), and memos
  • Life log item settings
    Item names, colors, and order
  • Display information for deleted items
    Names and colors of deleted items are kept on your device so that past logs can still be displayed correctly

This data is not sent to any server and is not tied to any account; it is stored only on your device (local storage).

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

By itself, the App does not send any information that identifies you to a server, nor does it automatically collect such information, other than the local data described above.

2.3 Information You Provide Through the Contact Form

When you use the “Contact” option in the App, you are taken to a Google Form (a service provided by Google LLC) to submit your inquiry.

  • Inquiry type and message are required. Your email address and name are optional and are requested only if you wish to receive a reply.
  • What you enter (including any optional email address and name) is processed on Google’s servers through Google Forms. We (piedra tienda) use this information only to read and respond to your inquiry.
  • For how Google handles data in Google Forms, please see Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Where Data Is Stored and Sharing

  • Life log data and related information are stored only on your device.
  • The provider of this App (piedra tienda) does not receive your life logs or memo content on any server, and does not provide such data to third parties.
  • We do not share your data with third parties without your consent, except where required by law (e.g., in response to lawful requests).

4. Advertising (When Ads Are Used)

When the App displays advertisements (e.g., Google AdMob), the following may apply:

  • Ad providers (e.g., Google LLC) may collect and use information such as device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data.
  • For details on such collection and use, please refer to each ad provider’s privacy policy.
    Google Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when we change whether we show ads or which ad providers we use.

5. Deleting Your Data

  • Life logs and item settings are removed from your device when you uninstall the App.
  • You can also delete individual logs or items using the delete actions within the App.

6. Children’s Use

This App is not directed at children under 13. If a child under 13 uses the App, we recommend that a parent or guardian consent to such use.

7. Changes to This Policy

  • We may revise this policy from time to time (e.g., when we change the App or to comply with law).
  • If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App or in the store listing. Your continued use of the App after changes will be treated as acceptance of the updated policy.
