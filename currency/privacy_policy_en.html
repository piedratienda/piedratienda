1. Introduction

Thank you for using "Currency" (the "App"), a mobile application provided by piedra tienda ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle user information in connection with the App.

The App is a currency conversion tool that allows you to convert one base amount into multiple currencies at a glance. No account registration is required, and the App does not collect any information that identifies you as an individual.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any information that identifies you as an individual (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos).

The amounts you enter, the base currency you select, your displayed currency list, input mode, theme setting, and language setting are stored only on your device. This information is never transmitted to our servers or to any external cloud service.

To reduce network requests and to allow the most recent rates to be shown offline, fetched exchange rates are cached in an on-device database (Hive) on your device.

All of this information is removed from your device when you uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Exchange Rate Retrieval (Frankfurter API)

To fetch up-to-date exchange rates, the App communicates with a third-party API called "Frankfurter" (https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/).

The only information transmitted in this communication is a three-letter currency code representing your base currency (such as JPY or USD). No information identifying you, no device identifiers, and no location data are transmitted.

However, due to the nature of network communication, your originating IP address reaches Frankfurter's operator. We do not collect or store this IP address, but its handling is under the control of Frankfurter's operator.

Frankfurter is an independent third party from us. For details, please refer to the official Frankfurter site (https://www.frankfurter.app/docs/).

3.2 Advertising (Google AdMob, Planned for the Future)

In the future, in order to cover the operating costs of continuing to provide the App free of charge, we plan to introduce advertising functionality provided by Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC).

When introduced, Google LLC may collect the following information:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Device information (OS, model, language, screen resolution, network type, etc.)
  • Approximate location (country/region level estimated from your IP address)
  • Information about ad impressions and clicks

Before introducing advertising functionality, we will amend this Policy and provide advance notice within the App and on this page. If the advertising feature has not yet been implemented at the time you are reading this Policy, the information collection described above is not taking place.

For details on how Google handles this information, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any information that identifies you as an individual, it can be used safely regardless of the user's age.

If a child under the age of 13 wishes to use our contact email address, please do so with the consent of a parent or guardian.

When Google AdMob is introduced in the future, we will operate it in accordance with Google's policies regarding child-directed content.

5. Data Security

To protect your information, the App takes the following measures:

  • Information you enter and your settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • All communication with third-party services takes place over encrypted HTTPS.

Please note that the security of your device itself (loss, theft, unauthorized access, etc.) is the responsibility of the user.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may amend this Policy due to changes in laws and regulations, additions of features to the App, or other reasons as needed. The amended Policy takes effect upon being posted on this page.

When making material changes (such as adding categories of collected information or adding third-party transmissions), we will provide advance notice by reasonable means, such as in-app notification at launch.
